HERITAGE
Agra, Uttar Pradesh

Taj Mahal

A world-famous marble mausoleum commissioned by Mughal emperor Shah Jahan. It is celebrated for its architecture and craftsmanship.

🏛️ Monument 📸 Photography
ROYAL HERITAGE
Jaipur, Rajasthan

The Pink City

Famous for its pink-colored historic buildings, royal palaces, forts, handicrafts and traditional Rajasthani culture.

🏰 Palaces 🛍️ Shopping
CULTURE
Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh

Varanasi Ghats

An ancient city known for its riverfront ghats, spiritual traditions, temples and historic cultural atmosphere.

🛕 Spirituality 🌅 Riverfront

Kerala Backwaters

🚤 Houseboats 🌿 Nature

Preserving Our Past

India's Rich Historical Heritage

India's heritage reflects centuries of artistic achievements, architectural innovation and cultural exchange. Its forts, palaces, temples, mosques and archaeological sites preserve stories from different periods of history.

Popular heritage experiences include:

  • Exploring ancient forts and royal palaces.
  • Learning about historic monuments and empires.
  • Discovering traditional crafts and architecture.
  • Visiting museums and archaeological sites.
  • Respecting and helping preserve heritage locations.

Colorful Cultural Festivals

Experience India's traditions through music, dance, food and celebrations.

Diwali

The Festival of Lights is celebrated with lamps, decorations, sweets, family gatherings and fireworks in many parts of India.

🪔 LIGHTS • SWEETS • CELEBRATION

Holi

The Festival of Colors welcomes spring through colorful powders, music, dance and joyful gatherings.

🎨 COLORS • MUSIC • JOY

Onam

Kerala's harvest festival is associated with floral decorations, traditional feasts, cultural performances and boat races.

🌼 CULTURE • FEASTS • TRADITION

Classical Arts Festivals

Across India, music and dance festivals celebrate classical traditions, regional performances and artistic expression.

🎶 MUSIC • DANCE • ART

Pongal

Celebrated especially in Tamil Nadu, Pongal gives thanks for the harvest through traditional dishes, decorations and community gatherings.

🌾 HARVEST • FOOD • GRATITUDE

Regional Fairs and Festivals

Regional celebrations showcase local handicrafts, folk dances, traditional clothing, food and music.

🎭 FOLK ART • CRAFTS • CULTURE
